DO NOT BEGIN RESEARCH ACTIVITIES BEFORE OFFICIAL IRB APPROVAL.

No recruitment, consent, surveys, interviews, focus groups, observations, distribution of research instruments, access to research records for the study, or data collection may begin until official PG IRB approval has been received.

Parallel progression model

Paper approval and IRB preparation move together

All students begin together. After formal paper review, students may continue toward IRB readiness or remain on the paper revision track. This center intentionally does not include an extension/repeat decision point.

IRB READY TRACK

Approved or explicitly cleared to move forward.

  1. Committee approval / clearance
  2. Mock IRB packet readiness review
  3. Faculty/RPC feedback and corrections
  4. Official PG IRB application when authorized
  5. Save submission confirmation
  6. Wait for IRB review / respond to requested revisions
  7. Official IRB approval
PAPER REVISION TRACK

Additional revision or approval is still needed.

  1. Continue paper revision
  2. Address faculty / committee / RPC feedback
  3. Use remaining formal paper review opportunities
  4. Keep IRB working drafts aligned with the current paper
  5. When approved, move to the IRB Ready Track
Only three formal paper review opportunities are built into ED825. Use each review deliberately and keep your IRB materials synchronized with the most current version of Chapters 1–3.

Official submission readiness

Submission is a milestone—not approval

Use this section only after Chapters 1–3 are approved/cleared, the mock packet has been reviewed, required corrections are complete, and you have authorization to proceed.

1

Confirm authorization

Verify that you have approval or explicit clearance to proceed and have addressed the mock packet review feedback.

2

Finalize the packet

Confirm the current protocol, participant-facing materials, recruitment, instruments, CITI, site permission, and supporting documents are complete and aligned.

3

Use the PG IRB Application Form

Complete candidate information carefully, select your Research Project Coordinator in the Faculty/Supervisor field, select the proposed pathway, and upload the required documents.

4

Send yourself a copy

Check “Send me a copy of my responses” before submitting so you receive the confirmation needed for the ED825 course dropbox.

5

Save confirmation

Save the confirmation email and upload it to the IRB Submission Confirmation Dropbox as directed in the course.

6

Wait for official approval

Monitor Purdue Global email, RPC communication, and IRB feedback. Respond to requested revisions, but do not begin research activities until official approval is received.

AFTER OFFICIAL IRB APPROVAL

Follow exactly what was approved.

Use only the approved recruitment materials, consent/information sheet, instruments, procedures, confidentiality protections, data-security procedures, and retention plan. If a change is needed, contact your faculty member or RPC before making the change because additional IRB review may be required.
